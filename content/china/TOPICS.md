# Mainland Unpacked: topic pillars and backlog

**Style:** documentary explainer, like The B1M (megaprojects: calm, confident narration; scale comparisons; "why it matters"), Bloomberg Originals, DW Documentary and CNA Insider. The arc is a hook, then one clear question, then data, then the human angle, then a takeaway.
- Every claim is fact-checked with a web search before scripting.
- Company figures are labeled as claims.

**Weekly mix (7 Shorts):** about 3 tech/build, 2 economy/geopolitics, 2 society. Never two Shorts on the same pillar on consecutive days.

## Pillars
1. **Tech and chips:** Huawei, SMIC, 3D chips / chiplets, AI models (DeepSeek, Qwen), robotics, drones (DJI), space.
2. **Cars and EVs:** BYD, NIO battery swap, Xiaomi SU7, EV exports, price wars, ghost-EV lots.
3. **Megaprojects and cities:** towers, bridges, high-speed rail, dams, new cities, Shenzhen-style growth, ghost cities.
4. **Trade wars and Made in China:** tariffs, export controls, rare earths, Made in China 2025, factory relocation (Vietnam, Mexico), Temu / Shein, Yiwu.
5. **Food:** feeding 1.4 billion with about 7–9 % of the world's farmland, pig towers, vertical farms, seed vaults, soybean imports, grain reserves.
6. **Energy:** solar overcapacity, Three Gorges, UHV power lines, coal vs renewables, nuclear build-out, the battery supply chain.
7. **Work and society:** 996, lying flat (tang ping), youth unemployment, gaokao and student pressure, involution (neijuan), dating and marriage costs, ageing.

## Backlog (pick, research, script; remove once used)
- Rare earths: why China controls about 90 % of processing
- Pig skyscrapers: a 26-floor pig farm in Hubei
- Solar: China makes more than 80 % of the world's panels; what happens to prices
